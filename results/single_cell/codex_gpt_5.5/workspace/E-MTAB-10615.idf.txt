Protocol Description	Four days before the time series start point, 8*10^5 passage 6 U3065MG cells were infected with the BFP expressing Perturbseq lentivirus at a MOI of < 0.1. Cells were incubated with the lentivirus as a single cell suspension in 20 µl of media for 4 hours at 37°C and seeded onto a laminin coated 60 mm dish. The next day, virus containing media was replaced with fresh media.At experimental day 0, 2500 BFP positive U3065 cells were sorted into one well on a 96 well plate coated with laminin. Cells were allowed to grow under stem cell conditions, and culture media was changed at day 3, 10 and 17. At experimental day 7 and 14, all cells were detached and 80% of the cells frozen down in media with 10% DMSO in -150°C, while the remaining 20% were seeded into a new well. After 21 days at the experimental endpoint, all cells were frozen down.	Cells were washed in 0.1% BSA in PBS before library preparation with the Chromium Single Cell 3’ GEM, Library & Gel Bead Kit v3 according to the manufacturer’s instructions.	Chromium Single Cell 3’ GEM, Library & Gel Bead Kit v3.	Libraries were pair-end sequenced usin a NextSeq 500 sequencer (Illumina).
